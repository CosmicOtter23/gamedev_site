I initially developed my programming skills by making websites and Unity games and they were further enhanced by studying computer science at university. These skills helped me learn Unreal Engine 5, which I used to create game prototypes in my games design master’s degree. I have always been passionate about video games and enjoy using my creativity to come up with new mechanics to design that test my skills. My strengths lie in the process of deducing the purpose of an aspect of gameplay and creating systems that reflect this through player feel and in-game outcomes.
