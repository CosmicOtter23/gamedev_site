Having enjoyed playing the game Marvel Rivals, and being a fan of Marvel in general, I decided I would make my own characters that could be in the game. The character that I had the most ideas for was Nightcrawler, an X-Men character that can teleport by travelling through an alternate dimension. I started by finding the character model on Unreal’s marketplace that looked most like the character Nightcrawler, then altered the materials to look more accurate to the character. I had conceptual ideas for how his character might be played in the game, so began experimenting with some mechanics.

The first ability I created was the recall. This was influenced by the fact that Nightcrawler can teleport to any place has seen before and adapted into gameplay by allowing the player to teleport to any position they were in within the last few seconds, depending on how long they hold the button. I then decided the character needed an engagement tool as well as an escape. I created an ability that allows them to teleport to a location a certain distance in front of them. I made these two abilities more visually exciting by adding particle effects and other visuals, making Nightcrawler leave blue clouds behind when he disappears and reappears. At this point, I added a primary attack for the character. I thought it made sense for him to have melee attacks as he can quickly close the distance and then escape. In the film X2, Nightcrawler attacks mostly with his feet, so I made his primary attack a series of kicks. After this, I made his ability to grab and enemy with his tail, allowing him to take them with him on his next teleport. Finally I gave him an ultimate ability that reduces the cooldown on his teleport to 2 seconds and makes him leave damaging clouds behind with each teleport.
