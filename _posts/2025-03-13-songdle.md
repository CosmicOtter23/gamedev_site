In the summer following my first year at university, I decided to test and try to improve the web development skills I had learned. I did this by trying to implement an idea I had for a different version of the Wordle game that had been popular at the time. This version sequentially gives the player 6 lines from a song, and they must guess the name of the song using as few lines as possible. I bought the songdle domain name, then deployed the website and kept it running for a year, with a new song each day. I shared the site on social media and with family members, and it ended up having hundreds of players every day, and over 30,000 different players in total. I took it down after a year because it was expensive to keep running it and time consuming to keep adding content to keep up with the demand of a new song daily, especially with university work to be doing.
