Between finishing my bachelor’s degree at The University of Leeds and my Master’s degree at The University of Central Lancashire, I got a job in data entry at a local manufacturing company. They needed someone with skills in computing to streamline the functionality of their databases. I began by rearranging spreadsheets to make the update and retrieval of data more efficient, and by creating better categories for the products on the website to fall into, improving user experience. The site used Shopfiy, which allows companies to sell their stock online without the need for an expert to create a functioning website. However, it allows manipulation of HTML to achieve specific layouts, so I was able to help with that; for example, my supervisor wanted drop shadows behind buttons on the website but there wasn’t an option for it on Shopify, so I altered the HTML and CSS to solve the problem.
