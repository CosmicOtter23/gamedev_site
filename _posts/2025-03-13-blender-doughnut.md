I used the COVID-19 lockdowns to teach myself a variety of skills online. One of these was the 3D modelling software Blender. As I had an interest in design and game development, I decided that it would be beneficial to me to know how to create 3D models. I followed a tutorial (https://youtu.be/nIoXOplUvAw?si=OICho5dfwI3IZZnu) on creating a doughnut. It was a good beginner’s tutorial as it taught me a good amount of a variety of different areas of the software; it covered the modelling, textures, colouring and rendering among other things. I learned a lot of new valuable skills that I have used in academic projects since.
