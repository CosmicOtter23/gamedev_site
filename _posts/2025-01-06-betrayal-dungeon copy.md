In this game, the player controls up to three characters, one of whom must reach the exit button to complete the level. The characters are controlled by the player mapping their path for them to follow on play. The difficulty is added when the player is faced with doors and buttons, forcing them to carefully plan out their character’s actions. 

More information about the design and development process can be found on my course <a href="https://lewiswraygamesdesign.blogspot.com/2025/01/game-features.html">blog</a>.
