This game began with some experimentation into game mechanics that could be fun to use as a player in an adventure game. I looked into games like Tomb Raider, God of War, Dishonored, and Star Wars Jedi to learn about individual mechanics that together make a cohesive and enjoyable gameplay experience. I used what I learned to design a system of energy manipulation, allowing the player to take energy from charged objects and place in into empty objects, making them interactable. The purpose of this project is to demonstrate how a mechanic can be used in all aspects of an adventure game including combat, puzzle solving, and level traversal. 

More information about the design and development process can be found on my course <a href="https://lewiswraygamesdesign.blogspot.com/2025/01/semester-2-project-progress.html">blog</a>.
