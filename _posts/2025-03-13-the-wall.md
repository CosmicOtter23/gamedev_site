When I first started my computer science degree at The University of Leeds, I wanted to maintain my game development skills, so I made a small game as practice. I made a version of the BBC game show “The Wall”, in which contestants must drop balls into a Plinko-style machine and answer questions, winning money for correct answers. I made a simple physics system, in which three balls fall from the top of the machine down to the money pockets at the bottom. While this happens, a trivia question in presented to the player, which they must choose an answer for before the balls reach the bottom of the machine. If they answer correctly, they add the money values for the balls to their total. If they guess incorrectly, the money is subtracted from their total. 

![Image 1](assets/img/the_wall1.jpg){:.post-image}

This game was comparatively simple and easy to make but I was happy with the result. Also, the project served as a helpful refresher in game making, and it was the first time I was able to explore the third dimension in games design.
