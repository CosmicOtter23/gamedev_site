I wanted to add some fun and whimsicality to my portfolio, while also making a game that allowed me to experiment with a variety of different types of mechanics. I took inspiration from the Worms series to make a similar game but with a top-down perspective. The games in the Worms series allow the player to choose from a menu of many weapons and abilities with vastly different effects. I used this as an opportunity to create a menu in my game, from which I can demonstrate one of many mechanics I have made. The game currently plays as a 2v2 local multiplayer game, and a player wins by destroying the other players 2 characters.

More information about the design and development process can be found on my course <a href="https://lewiswraygamesdesign.blogspot.com/2025/04/semester-3-design-documentation.html">blog</a>.
