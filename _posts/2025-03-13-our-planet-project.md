This was a large project in my A-level graphic design course that was based around activism. Students were asked to decide on a topic that interested them, so I focused on the subject of climate change. After working through some potential ideas, I finally decided on the design of the Earth being roasted over a fire. It was a simple visual that was still visually striking and conveyed the idea very clearly. I created the piece using Photoshop. It was initially in a cartoon style with bold black outlines around each shape but I changed it to look a bit more artistic. If I were to complete the work again, I think I could improve on it by straying further from the cartoonish style and making the fire and the melting planet look more realistic.
