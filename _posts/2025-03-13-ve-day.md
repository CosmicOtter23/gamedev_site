This project was for VE Day in 2020. Die to the recent lockdowns, much of my A-level graphic design course took place online, with all students working from home. I spent a lot of time in this period using Adobe Illustrator and thought my skills could be useful for this project. The assignment was to create a flag/bunting for the occasion. My initial idea was to alter the lines in the Union Jack flag into the 7 and 5 digits, as it was the 75th anniversary. It took some time to arrange the shapes and set it up clearly, so the 75 was prominent enough without obscuring the flag too much. I was proud of my design and it received some praise on social media.
