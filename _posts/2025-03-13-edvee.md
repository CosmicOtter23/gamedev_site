During my second year of university, I began looking for some work experience - either a placement year or a summer internship. I had a few interviews for placement years but was unsuccessful, then had an interview for a summer internship within The University of Leeds. I met with a member of staff who worked in product design and had an idea for a course design tool that he had tested with peers and found some success with. He needed a computer science student to adapt his process into a website so it is more automated, since he had been mapping out functionality on paper previously. I was hired for the internship, and started work in August 2023. I created a website using Python (Flask), SQL, JavaScript, and JSON for backend database functionality, and HTML and CSS for the design and layout. The internship was 10 weeks long, but my supervisor was able to secure additional funding for his project from the university so I could stay employed to do maintenance and improvements.
